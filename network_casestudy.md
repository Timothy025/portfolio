# Network CSMS – UX Case Study

## Overview

Network is a Charge Station Management System (CSMS) designed to help Charge Point Operators manage and monitor EV charging operations from a single platform. The ecosystem includes a web platform for operational management and a mobile application that allows EV users to start charging sessions by scanning a QR code or entering the Charger Point ID (CPID).

The goal of the platform was to simplify charging operations for both operators and EV drivers while improving visibility, accessibility, and ease of use across the entire charging journey.

---

# The Challenge

Managing EV charging infrastructure involves multiple workflows happening at the same time. Operators needed to monitor charging stations, track sessions, handle customer support, manage finances, and generate reports without feeling overwhelmed.

The existing experience had several usability gaps:

- Important information was scattered across different sections
- Monitoring charging sessions required too many steps
- Finance and reporting workflows felt complicated for non-technical users
- Users struggled to identify charger status quickly
- Customer management lacked clarity and easy accessibility
- Mobile users faced friction while starting charging sessions
- First-time EV users found the charging process confusing

The biggest challenge was reducing operational complexity while making the entire experience feel intuitive and stress-free.

---

# UX Goals

The design process focused on creating:

- A simpler and more organized operational experience
- Faster access to critical information
- Better visibility of charger and session status
- A smoother charging journey for mobile users
- Reduced cognitive load for operators
- Consistent experiences across web and mobile platforms

---

# Key Solutions

## 1. Simplified Dashboard Experience

The dashboard was redesigned to surface the most important information first.

Instead of overwhelming users with dense tables and multiple widgets, the interface prioritized:

- Live charger status
- Active charging sessions
- Revenue overview
- Network performance
- Alerts and issues

Clear visual hierarchy helped operators identify problems instantly without needing to navigate through multiple screens.

---

## 2. Improved Charging Session Monitoring

Charging session tracking was redesigned to make monitoring easier and faster.

The improved experience included:

- Real-time session visibility
- Cleaner session timelines
- Easy access to charging details
- Clear charging states such as Available, Charging, Faulted, and Offline
- Better filtering and search options

Operators could now identify ongoing issues in seconds instead of manually checking multiple records.

---

## 3. Better Finance & Reporting Experience

Financial workflows were simplified to improve readability and reduce confusion.

The redesigned reporting experience focused on:

- Easy-to-understand summaries
- Visual representation of revenue and usage
- Simplified report generation flow
- Reduced dependency on complex tables
- Faster access to downloadable reports

This helped non-technical users confidently access operational and financial insights.

---

## 4. Streamlined Customer Management

Managing customers and charging activity was redesigned to feel more connected and organized.

The improved experience included:

- Unified customer profiles
- Easier session history tracking
- Better visibility of charging behavior
- Simplified support workflows

Operators could now quickly understand customer activity without switching between multiple sections.

---

## 5. Improved Mobile Charging Experience

The mobile app experience was redesigned to reduce friction during charging initiation.

The focus was on making charging simple even for first-time EV users.

### Improvements included:

- Faster QR scanning flow
- Easier CPID entry
- Clear charging instructions
- Reduced steps to start charging
- Better charging status visibility
- Cleaner payment and session feedback

The overall experience became more confidence-driven and less intimidating for users unfamiliar with EV charging systems.

---

## 6. Consistent Design Language

A unified design system was introduced across the platform to create consistency between the web platform and mobile app.

This improved:

- Navigation familiarity
- Interaction consistency
- Visual clarity
- User confidence

The experience felt more connected and predictable across all touchpoints.

---

# Impact

The redesigned experience improved operational efficiency and usability across the platform.

### Key outcomes included:

- Faster monitoring of charging operations
- Reduced time spent identifying charger issues
- Improved accessibility for non-technical users
- Smoother onboarding for new EV drivers
- Reduced friction in starting charging sessions
- Better visibility into operational and financial data
- More intuitive navigation across the platform

The platform became easier to learn, easier to use, and more scalable for growing charging networks.

---

# Learnings

## Designing for Operational Simplicity Matters

Users managing charging infrastructure often work under pressure. Simplifying workflows had a major impact on usability and confidence.

---

## Clarity is More Important Than Feature Density

Adding more information does not always improve the experience. Prioritizing the right information at the right moment significantly improved usability.

---

## First-Time User Experience is Critical

Many EV users are still new to public charging systems. Reducing confusion during the charging process helped build trust in the product.

---

## Consistency Builds Confidence

Maintaining a consistent interaction pattern across web and mobile experiences helped users navigate the platform more comfortably.

---

# Conclusion

Network was redesigned with a strong focus on usability, operational clarity, and customer convenience. By simplifying complex workflows and improving the charging experience for both operators and EV drivers, the platform evolved into a more intuitive and scalable EV charging ecosystem.
